Support — Foothold

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Have a question or ran into an issue? Check the FAQ below, or email us directly at furkando01@gmail.com.

Where are my predictions stored?

In your own iCloud account, privately. Foothold does not operate its own server — see our Privacy Policy.

Why can't I change my prediction for a match?

Once a match's kickoff time has passed, predictions lock automatically. This keeps the competition fair for everyone.

What happens to my prediction if a match is postponed?

If a match is postponed or its kickoff date changes, your previous prediction is reset. You'll need to submit a new prediction before the new kickoff time.

What's the invite code for when I create a group?

Share your group's invite code with friends so they can find and join it using "Join with Invite Code." Only share it with people you trust — anyone with the code can join.

If I remove someone from a group, can they rejoin?

No. A removed member cannot rejoin the same group using the same invite code.

How do I delete my data?

Remove the App from your device and clear Foothold's data from your iCloud settings. For group data, either delete the group (if you're the owner) or leave it. To remove your entry from the global ranking, email us at the address above.

Which leagues are supported?

Süper Lig, Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, and the UEFA Champions League. You can choose which ones to follow from the Profile tab.

Is the "Ranking" tab different from a group's leaderboard?

Yes. Groups are only visible to people who know the invite code. The "Ranking" tab is a single worldwide list visible to everyone who uses Foothold, regardless of group membership — your display name and points are public there. See our Privacy Policy for details.
